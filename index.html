How to read Pro

1

Top-down

Yearly and Quarterly define the higher-timeframe draw. Trade Monthly and Weekly in that direction.

2

Liquidity first

Target the nearest unswept external liquidity. Look for entries at internal liquidity (FVG, order block, equilibrium), not at the highs.

3

Who is driving

FIIs covering while retail sells supports the move. FIIs adding shorts into a rally is a warning.

4

Confirm

SMT across Sensex, Nifty and Bank Nifty plus the COT backdrop, then your intraday entry model.
